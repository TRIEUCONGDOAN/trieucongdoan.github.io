Payment schedules are agreed for each engagement. Upon full payment of applicable fees, the client receives the rights to the final project deliverables described in the agreement, excluding third-party tools and materials governed by their own licences.

Post-launch support is provided only where included in the project agreement. If a concern arises, both parties agree to first seek a practical resolution through good-faith discussion before pursuing any formal process.
