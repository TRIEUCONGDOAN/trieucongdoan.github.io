Smart Booking App

BACKGROUND & CONTEXT Service businesses—salons, repair shops, consultancies, fitness studios, medical practices—rely heavily on manual scheduling. Staff spend hours fielding calls, managing spreadsheets, and sending reminder texts. Customers face long wait times, missed appointments, and confusion around availability. This friction costs businesses revenue through no-shows, inefficient staff allocation, and lost customer trust. OBJECTIVES Build an Android booking app that eliminates scheduling friction by giving customers direct access to real-time availability, instant confirmation, and automated reminders. Reduce no-show rates, free staff from manual scheduling tasks, and create a professional, modern touchpoint for customer interaction. CHALLENGES • Integrating with diverse backend scheduling systems • Ensuring real-time sync across multiple staff and locations • Handling timezone and availability complexity • Building trust through transparent, reliable booking flows KEY FEATURES • Real-time availability calendar synced to business operations • One-tap booking with instant confirmation • Automated SMS/push reminder notifications • Customer profile and appointment history • Staff dashboard for managing availability and appointments • No-show tracking and customer communication IMPACT Businesses report 25–40% reduction in no-shows, 50% decrease in scheduling-related staff time, and significantly improved customer satisfaction. The app becomes a trusted channel for repeat bookings.

Travel Planning App

BACKGROUND & CONTEXT Travel planning is fragmented and exhausting. Inspiration lives on Pinterest and Instagram. Booking confirmations arrive via email and SMS. Maps and guides scatter across apps. Itineraries exist in notes or Google Sheets. Travelers repeat the same research for overlapping trips and lose continuity across preparation phases. Uncertainty before departure creates stress and missed opportunities for better experiences. OBJECTIVES Build a unified travel companion that centralizes inspiration, planning, bookings, and preparation into one trusted space. Help travelers research faster, build better itineraries, and arrive prepared and excited—not stressed. CHALLENGES • Aggregating inspiration from diverse sources and formats • Coordinating multi-day itineraries across destinations • Integrating booking confirmations from various providers • Providing contextual information (weather, language, local insights) • Supporting offline access in areas with poor connectivity KEY FEATURES • Destination exploration with curated guides and inspiration • Multi-day itinerary builder with time-based scheduling • Booking management: flights, hotels, activities, restaurants • Interactive maps with offline support and local information • Packing lists and travel checklists • Travel journal with photo organization • Traveller community and real-time tips from recent visitors • Currency converter and practical travel information • Weather forecasts and activity recommendations IMPACT Travellers report 50% faster trip research, more confident bookings, better-prepared departures, and richer travel experiences. Post-trip, many use the journal and photos as lasting memory, encouraging app retention and repeat use for future trips.
